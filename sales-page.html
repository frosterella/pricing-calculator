Every piece gets a verdict

You'll know exactly what to do with each piece

The calculator sorts your work into three groups, so you stop guessing and start deciding.

Keeper

After costs and overhead, this piece earns at least the hourly rate you want. Make more of it.

Example: earns $38/hr against a $35 goal

Raise the price

It earns 60–99% of your rate. A price increase or a faster process will probably fix it.

Example: earns $27/hr against a $35 goal

Rethink this piece

It earns less than 60% of your rate. Raise the price a lot, make it faster, or keep it as a "love it" piece, not a "money" piece.

Example: earns $14/hr against a $35 goal

Questions

Before you join

I'm terrible at maths. Can I still do this?

Yes. You type your numbers into the yellow boxes and the calculator does the rest. If you can look up what you paid for supplies, you can do this.

Won't raising my prices scare off my customers?

The course helps you see which pieces need a higher price and by how much, so you can raise them with a reason. Some pieces may already be Keepers. Others might be better made faster or kept as "love it" pieces.

How long does it take?

The calculator takes about 30 minutes once you have your numbers. [ADD TOTAL COURSE TIME]

Does it work for my medium?

If you make physical pieces and sell them, yes. The calculator has fields for materials, components and hardware, firing and energy, packaging, shipping and more, and you can leave out anything you don't use.

I only sell a few pieces a year. Is it too early?

This is the best time. Setting your prices properly now means you won't have to fix them later, when you have more customers watching.

Is this tax or accounting advice?

No. It's a planning tool that helps you price your work and plan your income. Talk to an accountant about tax.
